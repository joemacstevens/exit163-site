Exit 163 Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Exit 163 is a local Bergen County newsletter and media brand. We value your privacy and keep this policy simple.

What we collect

  • Email address when you subscribe
  • Basic engagement data (opens/clicks) to improve content
  • Optional information you send us directly
  • If you text our number: your phone number, your messages and our replies, and anything you choose to tell us, such as your first name, town, email and what you like to do
  • If you're a business that claims a listing or pays to add a deal: your name, business and contact details (payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card)

How we use it

  • Send the Exit 163 newsletter and updates
  • Improve local content relevance
  • Operate and secure our service

Text messages

  • Texting our number starts a conversation with Exit 163's automated assistant, which uses AI (Anthropic's Claude) to answer from our list of local events and places. Replies can be wrong; check the source before you go.
  • If you add your number on our website, we text you once to confirm. Nothing else is sent until you reply JOIN.
  • We only send you the weekly text if you reply JOIN. About 1 message per week, sent between 8am and 9pm Eastern. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts, or HELP for help.
  • We only add you to the email newsletter if you text us your email and ask to be signed up.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties.
  • We may use what people ask about, combined and without names or numbers (for example, "lots of people asked about Ridgewood brunch"), to improve our guide and in reports to local businesses.
  • A reply may include one deal from a local place, on its own line starting "Deal:". Places pay us to attach deals. A deal never changes which places we recommend, and deals are never sent in the weekly text. The place sees the code you show, never your number.

Advertising cookies

Our website uses the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta (Facebook and Instagram), to measure how many people sign up after seeing our ads and to show our ads to people likely to be interested. Meta may set cookies and receive information about your visit, such as pages viewed and that a signup happened. We never send Meta your phone number or email. You can control ad personalization in your Meta ad preferences or block third-party cookies in your browser.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may use service providers (email delivery, text messaging, AI, payments, database and hosting) only to run Exit 163.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe anytime using the link in any email
  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts
  • Request deletion of your data, including your text history, by contacting us
