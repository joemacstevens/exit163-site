Exit 163 Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 23, 2026

Exit 163 is a local Bergen County newsletter and media brand. We value your privacy and keep this policy simple.

What we collect

  • Email address when you subscribe
  • Mobile number, if you sign up for texts, plus the messages you send our text line and its replies
  • Your home town, if you tell the agent
  • Basic engagement data (opens/clicks) to improve content
  • Optional information you send us directly

How we use it

  • Send the Exit 163 newsletter and updates
  • Answer your texts and send the weekly Friday text if you reply JOIN
  • Improve local content relevance
  • Operate and secure our service

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may use service providers (email delivery, analytics, hosting) only to run Exit 163.

Text messages

Texts are about 1 a week (plus replies to messages you send), sent 8am to 9pm Eastern. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase. Mobile numbers and text opt-in data are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe anytime using the link in any email
  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts
  • Request deletion by contacting us
