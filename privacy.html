Exit 163 Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026. Last updated: November 20, 2026.

Exit 163 is a local Bergen County newsletter and media brand. We value your privacy and keep this policy simple.

What we collect

  • Email address when you subscribe
  • Basic engagement data (opens/clicks) to improve content
  • Optional information you send us directly
  • If you text our number, by SMS or by RCS (rich messaging): your phone number, your messages and our replies, delivery and channel information (for example, whether a message was sent as SMS or RCS, and whether it was delivered or read), and anything you choose to tell us, such as your first name, town, email and what you like to do
  • If you're a business that claims a listing or pays to add a deal: your name, business and contact details (payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card)

How we use it

  • Send the Exit 163 newsletter and updates
  • Improve local content relevance
  • Personalize what we recommend to you (see "What we remember" below)
  • Operate and secure our service
  • Deliver texts over SMS or RCS, including the RCS features described under "Text messages"

Text messages

These terms cover ordinary SMS and RCS (sometimes called rich messaging or chat) when your phone and carrier support it. If RCS is not available, we send an SMS instead.

  • Texting our number, by SMS or RCS, starts a conversation with Exit 163's automated assistant, which uses AI (Anthropic's Claude) to answer from our list of local events and places. Replies can be wrong; check the source before you go.
  • When a message is delivered as RCS, your messaging app may show Exit 163 as a verified sender (our name, logo, and a verified-sender mark), and it may show typing indicators and read receipts. We may receive a notice that you opened a message. You can usually turn off read receipts, typing indicators, or RCS itself in your phone's messaging settings; doing that does not opt you out of Exit 163 texts — reply STOP to do that.
  • To deliver SMS and RCS, our messaging provider (Twilio) and, for RCS, Google (RCS Business Messaging) and your wireless carrier process your number, the message content, and delivery metadata. That processing is only to operate the service. It is not a use of mobile information or opt-in data for marketing or promotional purposes by third parties or affiliates.
  • If you add your number on our website, we text you once to confirm. Nothing else is sent until you reply JOIN.
  • We only send you the weekly marketing text if you reply JOIN. About 1 message per week, sent between 8am and 9pm Eastern. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Consent to receive marketing texts is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts, or HELP for help.
  • We only add you to the email newsletter if you text us your email and ask to be signed up.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties.
  • We may use what people ask about, combined and without names or numbers (for example, "lots of people asked about Ridgewood brunch"), to improve our guide and in reports to local businesses.
  • A reply may include one deal from a local place, on its own line starting "Deal:". Places pay us to attach deals. A deal never changes which places we recommend, and deals are never sent in the weekly marketing text. The place sees the code you show, never your number.

What we remember

  • When you text us, our assistant remembers a few short facts you share or show in your questions, so its picks fit you better: for example your town, how many kids you have and roughly how old they are (never their names), and what you're into, such as live music or date nights.
  • Each night an AI review of new messages tidies those facts, adding what was missed and removing what's out of date.
  • We use them for one thing: choosing what to recommend to you, in replies and in a "picked for you" line in your Friday text or email. We don't use them for ads, and we don't sell or share them.
  • We don't save health, religion, politics, money matters, last names or addresses, even if they come up.
  • Text MEMORY to see what we remember. Text FORGET to erase it, along with your chat history; you'll stay subscribed unless you also reply STOP.

Analytics and advertising cookies

Our website uses Google Analytics, a service from Google, to understand how people use the site: which pages get visited, roughly where visitors come from, and which links and buttons get tapped (for example, a link to text our number, a link to an event or tickets, or starting a business listing claim). Google may set cookies and receive information about your visit, such as your browser, device, approximate location and pages viewed. We never send Google your phone number or email. You can learn how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Our website also uses the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta (Facebook and Instagram), to measure how many people sign up after seeing our ads and to show our ads to people likely to be interested. Meta may set cookies and receive information about your visit, such as pages viewed and that a signup happened. We never send Meta your phone number or email. You can control ad personalization in your Meta ad preferences or block third-party cookies in your browser.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may use service providers (email delivery, text messaging including SMS and RCS, Google for RCS Business Messaging, wireless carriers, AI, payments, website analytics (Google Analytics), database and hosting) only to run Exit 163.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe anytime using the link in any email
  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts
  • If you use RCS, you can also control read receipts, typing indicators, and RCS in your phone's messaging settings. That does not stop Exit 163 from texting you; reply STOP to stop texts
  • Request deletion of your data, including your text history, by contacting us
