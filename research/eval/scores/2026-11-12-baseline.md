## For the builder (ranked by turns fixed)

1. **Recurring listings bury the real answer in `search_events`** (about 7 turns). A recurring row counts as happening every day in its date range, results sort by start date, and the limit is 8. A Saturday nightlife search returns 61 rows; rows 1-28 are weekday happy hours and trivia; Don Coqui's DJ night is row 33. The Thanksgiving Eve show sorts near the end of 69. This is also why Steel Wheel Tavern (row 3) turns up in 14 of 29 conversations. Fix: weekday-filter recurring events, sort events dated in the window first, push happy hours and trivia last, raise the limit.
2. **Every-word keyword matching, split across two tools** (about 7 turns). No venue row contains "danc", so `search_places({"keyword":"DJ dancing"})` and the production searches "dancing DJ latin" and "dancing" return nothing while Don Coqui sits in both tables. "Giants watch party", "live music games arcade karaoke" and similar can never match. Substring matching over-matches too: keyword "run" returned happy hours and Dracula, and parkrun was row 10 of 21. Fix: any-word ranked matching on word starts, a small synonym map, one combined search, and a prompt rule to retry with a single word in both tools before saying "nothing".
3. **No sense of nearby** (about 6 turns). Town filters are exact; nothing ranks by distance; place results come back in table order. Phone turn 6 searched Fort Lee for a DJ and never Edgewater. A user near Englewood was sent to Mahwah. The teens persona saw the same 10 of 29 teen-tagged places three times. Fix: a `near` parameter with drive minutes, auto-include neighbors, rank places.
4. **"Nothing in the window" ignores the next match just outside it** (3 turns). Freestyle search stopped at Dec 31; Freestyle Flashback is Jan 16. Family NYE search skipped Dec 30 and never searched places (Sky Zone's noon NYE party is in a place description). Fix: return next upcoming matches when the window is empty.
5. **No clock** (2 turns). The context has the date, not the time. At 9pm Thursday the agent said Twin Door "has live entertainment tonight"; its Thursday set runs 6-9pm. Fix: add NJ time to the context; for "right now" always search today's events county-wide.
6. **Memory capture is uneven** (Fit in 11 conversations). A 15-year-old never saved; four conversations saved nothing; "near Teaneck" style towns dropped, one saved as a loose fact; a visiting mother's tastes saved as the subscriber's; borderline wording ("wheelchair user", "tight budget, about $20 for two", "planning wife's 40th in January").
7. **Follow-ups can't see earlier tool results** (1-2 turns). "I don't know their Saturday hours" for two places whose hours were returned two turns earlier.
8. **Voice slips** (about 10 conversations). Friday email offered twice on production; "Text me Thursday" said on a Thursday; three questions stacked in one reply; "plenty of names with 'dog' in them came up"; small unsupported colour ("a cover band for a 40s crowd", "easy with a stroller").
9. **Leads and misses aren't captured.** A DJ with a monthly 90s party in Hackensack, exactly what two other testers asked for, was sent to the claim form and nothing was stored.

## Calibration notes (keep these for re-grades)

How I applied the rubric where it left room. A re-grade should use the same choices.

- **Knowledge from outcomes.** Share = (hits + half of partials) / recommendation turns, ignoring `handled`. 85%+ = 4, 65-84% = 3, 40-64% = 2, 15-39% = 1, under 15% = 0. A conversation with only `handled` turns gets 4.
- **Usefulness scale.** 4 = full plan with day, time, cost and a link where needed. 3 = clear top pick, one detail missing and flagged. 2 = real leads but the person still has to research hours, time or fit. 1 = only "call these places". When a conversation is mixed I averaged across turns.
- **Honesty scale.** 4 = everything supported or flagged. 3 = one or two small unsupported touches stated as fact (crowd age, "easy with a stroller", "already has DJs"). A false "I haven't heard of any" that follows an empty tool result is charged to Knowledge and Usefulness, not Honesty. Drive-time estimates and "you can walk between them" for two addresses in the same downtown are treated as acceptable, not invented.
- **`wrong` was not used.** The closest call is right-now turn 2 (a set that had ended): the place row the agent saw supports "live entertainment Wednesday through Saturday" and it said to call, so I graded the turn `search_miss`, set Honesty to 3 and filed it under the clock issue.
- **Compound asks get one outcome.** "X or Y": `hit` if one is answered well and nothing false is said about the other (the unanswered half still goes to research). "X and Y" with one half missing: `partial`. A turn with both a retrieval miss and a data gap and nothing answered: `search_miss`, with both issues logged. A false "nothing" on one half when the database had it: `partial` with a `search_miss` issue (freestyle-80s turn 1).
- **`search_miss` vs `partial`.** If the agent said it knew of nothing fitting and offered a "closest thing" while a clearly better fit was in the database, that is `search_miss` even though the reply was usable (girls-night turn 1, phone turns 1-6).
- **Fact follow-ups** about something already recommended (tickets, price, parking) are graded as knowledge turns: `hit`, `partial` or `honest_miss`. `handled` is kept for name, memory, business and pushback turns.
- **Memory.** Saving nothing when tastes, ages or a town were stated lowers Fit by one. Access needs and budgets are allowed kinds in the prompt, so "wheelchair user" and "tight budget" are flagged as wording issues, not scored as sensitive-data violations.
- **This week's issue counts as a source.** The craft fair, the Hillsdale boutique, the piano trio and "DJs Saturdays" at High Line exist only in `lib/issue.json`, not the database, and were accepted.
- **Phone thread.** The transcript has 14 user turns (the brief said 15). Production logs only `[town/category] text`, so which tool each search used is inferred: a logged line that repeats the user's message means no keyword was passed.
- **Verification method.** `scripts/eval-lookup.js` for every "nothing" claim (several words each). For addresses, prices and hours, which that script doesn't print, I used a read-only select of full rows, and a read-only replay of the `search_events` ordering to get row positions. Nothing was written to the database and the search tools were not called directly, since they log a row.
- **Categories.** One primary category per conversation for the means; turn outcome counts use each turn's own topic.
- **Rounding.** Scores round half up.
