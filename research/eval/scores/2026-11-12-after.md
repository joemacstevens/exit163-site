It searched "menorah lighting" only. Nothing was saved, not even the town.

- **Honesty.** One contradicted price (rain-plan). Five smaller unsupported claims: "about 5 minutes from both of you" for Fair Lawn and Paramus (the tool says about 10 from Paramus); "Zina's and Don Coqui both just say 21+" (Don Coqui's rows give no age limit); "It's quiet and easy to talk there" about a gallery; "take the 9:30 set if you'd like it a little calmer"; "a fun crowd". Crowd and age claims now come from row tags ("40s to 60s", "40+", "a dressier lounge crowd") and the agent says "I can't promise the crowd" when there is no tag.
- **Bad fit.** No adults sent to kids' programs or the reverse; nothing outside Bergen; nothing already over (the "happening right now until 10" line-dancing night was correct). Requirements were dropped four times: "registration required" on the Teaneck music story time (toddler-weekday t2) and the teen library party (teens t2), "advance registration required, rain cancels" on the Ramapo hike (active t3), "payment due in full to reserve" on NYE bowling (nye t3). No "residents only" row was recommended.
- **Voice.** No reply over 750 characters (longest 725; mean 516 against 520 before). Replies are not more list-like overall: 33 replies use dashes against 45 before, though three are lists of four or five items. "The listing says" wording rose from 2 replies to 12.
- **Speed.** Four replies took over 10 seconds (one before). budget-two t3 took 14.4 seconds, past the 12-second SMS limit, and t2 took 11.6 with no tool call.
- **Memory.** See issue 6 below: better on ages, towns and wording; four conversations saved nothing.

## What still fails: builder

1. **Details land on the wrong thing or lose their conditions** (6 turns, including the `wrong` one). A price from a meetup event was applied to the venue. Descriptions are cut at 200 characters, usually just before "registration required". Saved rows keep only date, time and cost. Fix: a short `needs` field (registration, residents only, ages, RSVP, pay in full) that is never cut and is kept in the digest; mark event costs in cross-results as that event's only.
2. **A badly chosen keyword still misses rows, and saved rows let the agent skip a search** (4 turns). access t2 missed the one live show tagged wheelchair accessible; cultural t1 missed Teaneck's own events; freestyle-80s t2 never searched for tribute bands. Fix: make an access need a filter, search both words of a two-part ask, pass `include_date_tba` for holiday asks, and search again when something new is asked.
3. **Memory still uneven** (Fit in 6 conversations). Four conversations saved nothing; "near X" towns missed three times.
4. **Unsupported colour, "the listing says", one canned line** (Honesty in 5 conversations). Say which town a drive time is measured from; ban "listing"; vary the check-back wording.
5. **Slow replies and an unverified lead tool.** One reply at 14.4 seconds would be a dropped text. Apply the tickets migration and confirm a lead is written once.

## Calibration notes for this re-grade

Everything in the baseline's calibration notes was applied unchanged. Three points needed a decision the baseline didn't face:

- **When a misapplied detail is `wrong`.** The baseline kept `wrong` for contradicted facts. I applied it when a hard fact the person would act on (when, where, how much, whether they can get in) is contradicted by the data or lifted from a different row. The GOAT price qualifies. "Don Coqui says 21+" (untrue of the row, harmless to a 38-year-old) and "5 minutes from both of you" (off by about 5 minutes) were scored as Honesty 3, like the baseline's colour. Seasonal generalities with "usually" stay at Honesty 4, as in the baseline.
- **Dropped requirements.** A recommended item whose row says registration, payment or RSVP is required, with no mention in the reply, caps the conversation's Fit at 3. The turn becomes `partial` only when the reply leaves no other usable option (active t3, teens t2, nye t3); toddler-weekday t2 stays a hit because four other options stand.
- **Drive times.** "About N minutes" counts as supported when it matches the tool's `approx_drive_min` for the nearest town the person named. I recomputed each pair with the code's own formula.

Method notes:

- `scripts/eval-lookup.js` now misses rows: one request returns 1,000 of the 1,047 events (994 of 1,041 upcoming), it prints only the first 40 matches, and its "today" is the UTC date, so by evening it drops that day's events. I verified rows with a paginated read-only select instead. The script needs paging before the next grade. Nothing was written to the database and the search tools were not called.
- The agent sees descriptions cut at 200 characters. I graded what the subscriber was told against the full row, and filed the truncation under builder issue 1.
- Where a conversation's content is unchanged (noraebang, sports-fan, pushback, event-facts), each dimension keeps the baseline's reading so the comparison holds.
