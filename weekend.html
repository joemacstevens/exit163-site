Your Bergen weekend · Thanksgiving weekend, Nov 25-29
Home for the long weekend? Here's the plan, Wednesday to Sunday: where the Thanksgiving Eve reunions are, real options if you're not drinking, a Thanksgiving-morning 5K to earn the second plate, a 2000s dance party Friday in Rutherford, and the holiday lights switching on in Paramus.
Thanksgiving Eve: Wednesday, Nov 25
Start with East Rutherford, North Arlington and Carlstadt, then a few more spots around the county. These are bars, lounges and a brewery, so bring 21+ ID. Anything marked call to confirm wasn't posted when we checked Tuesday night.
1
DJ Anthony Pags — The Roosevelt, 356 Paterson Ave, East Rutherford. 8pm-1am. The Thanksgiving Eve DJ night at the saloon-and-speakeasy. The time comes from the DJ's own page; the venue calendar lists the date only. Cover not posted, call to confirm: (201) 713-3145. Bar, so bring 21+ ID. Venue calendar · DJ listing
2
Thanksgiving Eve Rock Night with Somethin' Darling — Shawn's Crazy Saloon, 350 Belleville Tpke, North Arlington. 9pm-1:30am. A live cover band for the sing-every-word crowd: Nickelback, Pearl Jam, Zeppelin, Muse. $16 early bird (limited) or $20 presale on Eventbrite, no refunds. Ages 21+ per the ticket page. Tickets
3
Thanksgiving Show: Dense Benson + Friends — Bolero Snort Brewery, 316 20th St, Carlstadt. Doors 7pm, music 8-11pm. Jauncey, Bonce and Dense Benson in a brewery taproom: an easier, earlier night. Price not posted, call to confirm: (201) 464-0639. Brewery, so bring 21+ ID; age policy not posted. Events
4
Drinksgiving with DJ P La Cangri at Cigarro — 91 Main St, Hackensack. 9pm-2am. A Hackensack nightspot. Afro house, Latin blends, throwbacks and R&B, pitched at the bring-your-out-of-town-crew crowd. Tickets are on Shotgun but weren't released yet Tuesday night, so the price is TBA. Tables through Resy or (201) 546-7000. Age not posted, so plan on 21+ with ID and call to confirm. The 2am close is from one listing only; the venue normally closes at midnight Wednesdays. Event · Tickets
5
Rubber Duckie Riot — Mason Jar, 219 Ramapo Valley Rd, Mahwah. 8-11pm. Live band night up north. Cover and age policy not posted, call to confirm: (201) 529-2302. Live music
6
Thanksgiving Eve Pub Party — The Shannon Rose. 9pm. A Thanksgiving Eve party with the kitchen open late and a reservation link on the page. The page doesn't say which Shannon Rose (there's one in Ramsey on Route 17 and one in Clifton), so call to confirm the location before you go. Cover and age not posted; pub, so bring 21+ ID. Event
Getting home? Line up a ride before you head out: pick a designated driver or have the rideshare app ready.
No Drinking Required: Wednesday
No drinking required. Under 21, driving, or just not into the bar scene:
Thanksgiving Eve Latin Social — Linguallama Academy, 1 Teaneck Rd, Ridgefield Park. 7pm to 11:30pm-ish. Salsa class at 7 (all levels, no partner needed), then DJs Rudy "El Profe" and Nico on salsa and bachata. $25 covers a Thanksgiving buffet and a complimentary coquito (traditionally made with rum, so under-21s should ask). BYOB, and only those 21+ drink. Dress code: fall colors. The two listings disagree on the end time (11:30pm vs 12:30am), and the age policy isn't posted, so call to confirm: 551-245-9171. Meetup · Eventbrite
Thanksgiving Eve bowling — Holiday Bowl, 29 Spruce St, Oakland. 3:30pm to midnight. Packages are prepaid by advance reservation only. No walk-ins. Price not posted and the hours come from a local roundup, so call to confirm: 201-337-6516. Holiday Bowl · Listing
Free Friendsgiving Line Dance — Elks Lodge, 200 Route 17, Paramus. Dancing 7:30-10:30pm. Free beginner lesson at 6pm, an optional $20 class at 6:30, then a potluck (bring a dish) and open dancing. You must pre-register through the Meetup page. Cash bar, and no outside alcohol. It's a singles group's event, open to adults. Meetup
Thanksgiving Morning: Turkey Trots
Thanksgiving morning, Thursday Nov 26. Check your race's page before you go: online signup closes the morning of some races, Leonia's price went up after Tuesday, and one Bergen trot (Fort Lee) has already been canceled over a severe-weather forecast for Thursday.
Upper Saddle River 5K — 9am, check-in 7:30am, Upper Saddle River. The 27th running. $40. Entries are online only; you can sign up on your phone when you arrive. usr5k.org
Racefaster Thanksgiving Day Run — 9am, 390 Doremus Ave, Glen Rock. 5K $45; kids' fun run 9:45am, $30. Online signup closes Thursday at 10:45am. Good for kids. Register
River Vale Turkey Trot Charity 5K — 8am, Mark Lane Sports Complex, 400 Mark Lane, River Vale. $35 plus fee for adults; the kids' price shows as both $20 and $25 on the page, so check registration. Online signup closes Thursday at noon; registration opens at 7am. Proceeds go to the Helping Hand Food Pantry. Good for kids. Register
Leonia Turkey Trot — 5K at 8am, 1-mile at 9am, 305 Beechwood Place, Leonia. Was $35; the price went up after Tuesday night and the new one isn't posted, so check registration. Day-of signup and check-in from 7am. Register
Friday Night, Nov 27
Love In The Club: 2000s Hip Hop & R&B Dance Party — Williams Center Black Box, 15 Sylvan St, Rutherford. Doors 8pm, show 9pm. The Y2K throwback night, built for a group. $19.28-$32.64. 18 and up per the Williams Center. Tickets on sale now. Tickets
Friendsgiving with Achievers & The Bones — Lenora's, 129 Johnson Ave, Hackensack. 8pm-11:55pm. A Grateful Dead / Jerry Garcia Band tribute with an opening set. Free admission per the listing; this is a bar and the age policy isn't posted, so call to confirm. Listing · Local Music Finder
Ping Pong Party — Ready to Golf, 3 New Bridge Rd, River Edge. 7:30-9:30pm. A Meetup group's ping pong night. About $10-15 a person for table rental (paddles and balls included). Bring cash. RSVP on Meetup. Meetup
Paint night: "Home Sweet Cardinal" — Pinot's Palette, 25 Oak St, Ridgewood. 7:30-10pm. $45, book a seat online. Classes
Pottersfield + Delta Screws, Withindyinglight and Interchange — CUG Cantina, Williams Center, Rutherford. 8:30pm. Four bands, $13.63, 18 and up per the venue. Tickets
The Lights Are On
Bergen County Winter Wonderland opens Friday — Van Saun County Park, 216 Forest Ave, Paramus. The county's outdoor skating rink and holiday village is back from Friday, Nov 27. Tickets are timed and some sessions sell out; skate rental is $5. 2026 hours and ticket prices weren't posted yet Tuesday night, so check the site before you go. Winter Wonderland
Zoo Lights: Let It Glow — Bergen County Zoo, 216 Forest Ave, Paramus. Thu-Sun, 5-9pm, from Fri Nov 27. Right next door to Winter Wonderland, so do both. $15 adults, $8 ages 3-17, one train ride included. Tickets on Eventbrite or at the booth; last entry 45 minutes before close. Good for kids. Zoo Lights
Orchard of Lights — Demarest Farms, 244 Wierimus Rd, Hillsdale. Through Dec 29. A 20-25 minute drive through the lights, tuned to 107.9 FM. $35 per car for the light show only, $59 with the petting zoo (up to four people), $69 VIP (up to six), each with a free Santa visit. Reservation required; no tickets are sold at the farm. Good for kids. Book
Hasbrouck Heights Holiday Parade & Tree Lighting — Friday, 7pm, The Boulevard (LaSalle Ave to the Passaic Ave Circle). Free, rain or shine; the tree gets lit at the Circle right after the parade. Good for kids. Details
