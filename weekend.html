Your Bergen weekend · Friday, Nov 20
The weekend before the rush. Thanksgiving is Thursday, so this is the last quiet-ish weekend before the holidays take over. The season's first big lights open, a 42-foot Santa lights up in Paramus, and the turkey trots are filling up.
This Weekend: Top 5
1
Big Santa 2026 — Sunday, 4-6pm, Westfield Garden State Plaza, Paramus. Santa arrives by fire truck, the 42-foot Big Santa lights up, and a fireworks show set to holiday music closes the night. Live sets from All-4-One, TKA, Spice Wannabe and Just Your Type, hosted by Ryan Cabrera, plus stilt walkers, face painters, balloon artists and food trucks. It's in the parking lot near Entrance 7, by Nordstrom. Free to book, but you need a spot: reserve here. Good for kids. Details
2
Orchard of Lights opens — Saturday, Demarest Farms, Hillsdale (through Dec 29). A 20-25 minute drive through the orchard's lights, tuned to 107.9 FM, plus fire pits, s'mores, the petting zoo and Santa (Santa and the farm market run until 9pm nightly through Dec 24). $59 per car covers the drive, petting zoo for up to four and a Santa visit. Reservations required and your time slot is set when you book; no tickets are sold at the farm, and no oversized vehicles. Good for kids. Book at orchardoflights.com
3
Christmas Fair — Saturday, 10am-4pm, Grace United Methodist Church, 555 Russell Ave, Wyckoff. Vendors, baked goods, face painting, funnel cakes and lunch, free crafts for kids, and pictures with Santa from 11am to 1pm. Free registration is optional. Good for kids. Church calendar
4
Cirque Musica Holiday Wonderland — Saturday, 2pm and 7pm, Dream Live at American Dream, East Rutherford. A holiday circus show, listed as all ages. Tickets on Ticketmaster (check face-value prices there; resale listings run higher). Good for kids. Tickets
5
Ridgewood Guild Winterfest — Saturday, 11am-3pm, Memorial Park at Van Neste Square, Ridgewood. Free. A trackless train, hayride, storytelling, music, a dance performance and Santa. The Guild says its times can shift, so call to confirm: (201) 493-9911. Good for kids. Winterfest

Turkey Trots: Sign Up This Week
Thanksgiving morning, Thursday Nov 26. Sign up this week — prices go up and spots run out as the day gets close.
Upper Saddle River 5K — 9am, Cavallini Middle School, 392 W. Saddle River Rd, Upper Saddle River. The 27th running. $40. Entries are online only — sign up at home or on your phone at check-in (7:30-8:45am). usr5k.org
Palisades Dock-to-Dock Turkey Trot — 9am, Ross Dock Picnic Area, Fort Lee. An untimed 5K fun run along the Hudson from Ross Dock to Englewood and back. $30, capped at 200 runners. Register
Racefaster Thanksgiving Day Run — 9am, 390 Doremus Ave, Glen Rock. 5K for $45, plus a kids' fun run at 9:45am for $30. Good for kids. Register
River Vale Turkey Trot Charity 5K — 8am, Mark Lane Sports Complex, 400 Mark Lane, River Vale. $35 plus fee, ages 13 and under $25 plus fee (the race page also lists older, lower prices; checkout shows these). Registration opens at 7am; online signup closes at noon Thanksgiving Day. Proceeds go to the Helping Hand Food Pantry. Good for kids. Register
Leonia Turkey Trot — 5K at 8am, 1-mile run at 9am, 305 Beechwood Place, Leonia. $35 for either; the price goes up after Tuesday, Nov 24. Check-in and day-of signup from 7am. Register
Pie Deadline
Demarest Farms, Hillsdale — order by Sunday, Nov 22. Apple or pecan $24.95, pumpkin $19.95, blueberry $26.95. Pick up Tuesday 11/24 or Wednesday 11/25 (call for pickup hours). Order on the farm's 2026 order form or by phone at (201) 666-0472, 244 Werimus Rd.
Family Pick
Celebrate Hanukkah with Chabad of Paramus — Sunday, 3-4pm, Paramus Public Library, 116 E. Century Rd. An early Hanukkah celebration (the holiday starts Dec 4) for ages 4 and up. Free registration required, and only a few seats were left Friday morning. Good for kids. Register
Sunday Morning: Ringside
Blue Collar Fight Day — Sunday, first bell 10am, Different Breed Elite Fitness, 401 Water St, Teaneck. Thirteen amateur boxing bouts, the finale of a six-week fight camp for everyday people. $5 at the door for nonmembers. Details
Friday Night, Grown-Ups Only
"The Player" (1992) — Friday, 5:15pm or 7:45pm, Cinema Underground at the Williams Center, Rutherford. Robert Altman's Hollywood satire on the big screen, with one more show Saturday at 5:15pm. 18 and up, $13.63-$15.69. Tickets Rather hear live music? Kyler Sane plays the Williams Center's CUG Cantina the same night: doors 7pm, show 8pm, 18 and up, $19.28. Events
