Your Bergen weekend · Friday, Nov 13
Welcome to Exit 163 — Bergen County's Friday weekend brief.
Every Friday morning: the week's best games, food, and things to do, in one quick read.
It's free, it's local, and this is issue number one.
This Weekend: Top 5
1
Finals night — Friday. All six Bergen football teams play for sectional titles tonight: Shabazz at Old Tappan, 6pm (10-0, chasing a three-peat); Middletown South at Northern Highlands, 6:15pm (Highlands' seventh straight final); Caldwell at Westwood, 7pm (the defending champs); and New Milford at Newton, Hasbrouck Heights at Cedar Grove, and Hoboken at Waldwick/Midland Park, all at 7pm. It's finals night for girls soccer too — Ramsey (19-1) hosts Montville at 6pm, chasing a fourth straight sectional title, and Waldwick pulls double duty on the same field: soccer at 2pm, football at 7pm. Rain all day and low 40s by kickoff — dress for it.
2
Meet of Champions — Saturday, Holmdel Park. The state's best runners close the cross-country season: boys at 11am, girls at noon. Bergen sends a full contingent, led by sectional champs New Milford's boys and Ramsey's Kaylee Finnegan.
3
"A Christmas Story" — Saturday, 7pm, bergenPAC, Englewood. The holiday season officially starts when Ralphie hits the stage. Get tickets before the Thanksgiving rush discovers it.
4
Paramus Catholic Craft Fair — Sunday, 10am-4pm, 425 Paramus Rd, Paramus. One of the county's biggest holiday craft fairs — 125+ vendors, $2 admission (kids under 12 free) — and the unofficial start of gift-buying season. Go early; the good stuff goes fast.
5
Piano Trio: "Exquisite Contrasts" — Sunday, 2:30pm, Forte Piano Music Company, 159 Route 4, Paramus. Haydn's "Gypsy" Trio from three award-winning players. A quieter way to close the weekend — chamber music before the holiday noise arrives.

Family Pick
Holiday Boutique Vendor Sale — Saturday, 10am-3pm, Hillsdale United Methodist Church, 349 Hillsdale Ave, Hillsdale. Free admission, local vendors, and an easy first holiday outing with kids in tow. Pair it with the craft fair Sunday and your gift list is half done before Thanksgiving.
Date Night
"Dracula: A Comedy of Terrors" — Bergen County Players, Oradell (final performances, through Saturday). A fast, funny Dracula — horror-comedy is a better date than straight horror, and this is your last chance before it closes. Dinner in Oradell or Westwood before the show and you're set.
Food and Biz
Rojo Tequileria & Mexican Grill is open in Cliffside Park — Anderson Avenue, in the old Viva Margarita spot. Latin-inspired menu and a private-event space.
Knockout Bagels is open in Ramsey — 475 North Franklin Turnpike. Locally owned, New York-style, baked fresh early. Ramsey finally has a bagel answer.
Also This Weekend
Free golf for veterans ends today. Bergen County's six courses have been free all week for veterans and active-duty members, cart fee waived — Friday is the last day.
Week Ahead
Wednesday Nov 18: High Line Piano Bar & Restaurant opens in Ridgewood — second floor above Steel Wheel Tavern on North Broad St. Jazz trios Wednesdays, DJs Saturdays. The county's date-night rotation just got deeper.
Saturday Nov 21: group semifinals — Friday's football winners play for spots in the state finals.
