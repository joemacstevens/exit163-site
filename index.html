Questions

Quick answers.

What is Exit163, exactly?

A buddy you text about what's up around Bergen County: things to do, places to go, what's open, what's on. It checks verified local listings before it answers and tells you where the answer came from. All 70 towns, Mahwah to Edgewater.

How many texts will I get?

One on Fridays, sent between 8am and 9pm. Everything else is Exit163 replying to something you sent it.

Phone, email, or both?

Your phone gets you the buddy: ask it anything, any day, plus the Friday text. Your email gets the Friday letter, the whole weekend in one read. Both is best, either works.

Is it really free?

Yes. Local places can get verified or add deals, and those are always labeled. Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

How do I stop?

Text STOP, or hit unsubscribe in any email. Done, instantly.
