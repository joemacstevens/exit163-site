Questions

Quick answers.

What exactly is the Exit 163 agent?

An AI that knows Bergen County. You text it like a person, and it searches our database of verified local events and places to answer. It covers all 70 towns, from Mahwah to Edgewater.

How many texts will I get?

About one broadcast a week, the Friday weekend text, sent between 8am and 9pm. Anything else is the agent replying to something you sent it.

Should I give my phone, my email, or both?

Both gets you the most. Texts are for quick answers and the Friday heads-up. Email is the full Friday issue with every section. But either one on its own works great.

Is it really free?

Yes. Local businesses can sponsor a town, and sponsored picks are always labeled. Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

What’s MCP?

Model Context Protocol, the standard way AI assistants connect to outside tools. If your assistant supports custom MCP connectors, paste https://www.exit163.com/mcp and it can look things up in Exit 163 directly.

How do I stop?

Text STOP for texts, or click unsubscribe in any email. Instant.

I own a business here. Can I get listed?

Yes. Claim your free listing or sponsor your town.
